Engineering Approach

Money and state stay honest Wallet and ledger writes are idempotent and queued, not fired directly from the request handler.
Modules own their domain Each NestJS module wraps one business concept, its DTOs, its guards, and its own tests.
Auth is layered, not bolted on JWT for API sessions, passkeys where phishing-resistant login matters, role guards on every protected route.
Third-party APIs get a seam Providers sit behind a service interface, so a provider swap is a rewrite of one file, not the whole codebase.
