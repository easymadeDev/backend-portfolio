Architectural Principles

01

Money stays honest

ACID compliance and strict ledger logic aren't optional. Financial data must be auditable and resilient to race conditions.

02

Modules own domain

Embracing Domain-Driven Design in NestJS to keep boundaries clear, reducing coupling and making services easier to reason about.

03

Auth is layered

Security at the gateway, controller, and service levels. Implementing modern standards like WebAuthn for robust identity verification.

04

APIs get a seam

Designing interfaces that abstract internal complexity, allowing frontend teams to consume endpoints predictably and safely.
